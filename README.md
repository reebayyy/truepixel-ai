<h1 align="center">TruePixel AI</h1>
<p align="center">An AI photo editor for Windows. Most AI runs on your PC, offline.</p>

<p align="center"><a href="https://github.com/reebayyy/truepixel-ai/releases/latest"><b>⬇ Download for Windows</b></a></p>

## Install

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/reebayyy/truepixel-ai/releases/latest) and download `TruePixel-AI-Setup-<version>.exe`.
2. Run it. Windows may show **"Windows protected your PC"** because the installer isn't code-signed yet
   (signing costs money; this is a student project): click **More info → Run anyway**.
3. Pick a folder, finish, and TruePixel AI opens.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). About 300 MB; some AI tools download their model the first time you use them.

## What it does

- **Edit** — light, colour, filters, crop & straighten, with full undo/redo history
- **AI on your PC** — remove the background, click to select people or objects, erase things, body-part and style templates
- **Describe an edit** — type what you want ("make it warmer and blur the background"); simple edits run offline,
  the rest can use an online AI model, only after you agree
- **Pose changes** — pick a pose template (online AI)
- **Sign in with Google (optional)** — your photos and their edit history sync across your PCs
